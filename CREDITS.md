# Credits

PROPHET 67 by swtdr34mz. All of its own code (the voices, the filters, the envelopes, poly mod, vintage and spread,
the effects and reverbs, the sequencer and arpeggiator, the panel and its screens) is written for this project. The
instrument it is modelled on is the Sequential Prophet-6 (Dave Smith); the SPACE reverb takes its idea from the Meris
Mercury7. No code, samples or artwork of either is used. Not affiliated with Sequential, Meris or M-VAVE.
